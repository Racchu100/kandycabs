Company profile

Fourteen years on these roads

Kandy Cabs started in 2012 with a single Indica and one driver running airport trips out of Ullala. Today we operate twelve vehicles across Mangaluru, Udupi and the wider coastal belt, with drivers who have run the Shiradi and Charmadi ghats through every monsoon since.

We are a family-run operator, not an aggregator. The person who answers the phone at midnight is the same person who owns the vehicle you booked. That is the whole difference.
